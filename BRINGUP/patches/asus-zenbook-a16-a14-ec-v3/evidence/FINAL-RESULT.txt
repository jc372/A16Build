ASUS Zenbook A16/A14 EC driver, v3 -- Konrad Dybcio, 2026-09-23
Tested on hardware.

TESTED ON
  ASUS Zenbook A16 (UX3607OA), Qualcomm Snapdragon X2 Elite (Glymur)
  linux-next next-20261002, CONFIG_EC_ASUS_GLYMUR=y (built in, not a module)

RESULT
  The driver binds, and everything the series claims works:

    i2c 9-0076 -> asus-glymur-ec     hwmon4   name=asus_glymur_ec
    i2c 9-005b    EC subdevice       led      asus::kbd_backlight

    fan1  1920-2040 RPM          temp1  36-38 C   (CPU)
    fan2  1320 RPM               temp2  38-41 C   (SoC)
    kbd backlight  1 of 3, and the backlight responds

  Suspend and resume both reach the EC:

    asus_glymur_ec_suspend   ret=0x0   488.593 us
    asus_glymur_ec_resume    ret=0x0   439.584 us

    i2c_write: i2c-9 #0 a=076 f=0000 l=2 [23-07]    0x23 STANDBY_CMD / 0x07 ENTER
    i2c_write: i2c-9 #0 a=076 f=0000 l=2 [23-08]    0x23 STANDBY_CMD / 0x08 EXIT

  A full s2idle cycle completes -- suspend_stats 1 -> 2 on a real suspend, and
  2 -> 3 under pm_test=devices -- with fail 0 and last_failed_dev empty.

NOT TESTED
  The event path, and nothing else.

NOTES ON THE TREE
  next-20261002, rather than the next-20260922 the series was rebased onto.  The
  board dts this series touches also carries unrelated local patches of ours
  (Bluetooth, RTC, an HDMI power-domain workaround); none touch the &i2c9 hunk.
  The series itself is unmodified -- driver and binding byte-identical to as
  posted, and the &i2c9 block 3/3 produces is identical to ours.
