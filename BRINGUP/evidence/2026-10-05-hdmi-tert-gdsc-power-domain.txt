HDMI on the A16 -- tertiary combo PHY power domain
2026-10-05, kernel 7.3.0-rc5-next-20261002-t2

MACHINE
  model        ASUS Zenbook A16 (UX3607OA)
  soc          Qualcomm Snapdragon X2 Elite (Glymur)
  kernel       7.3.0-rc5-next-20261002-t2
  monitor      MSI, 5120x1440, plugged into the HDMI connector

CAUSE
  The HDMI output is fed from the tertiary combo PHY (phy@88e1000) through the
  PS185 bridge.  That PHY's "com_aux" clock is parented to GCC_USB30_TERT_GDSC.
  No device-tree node consumes that domain -- the USB controller on the same PHY
  is not enabled on this board -- so the domain never powers on, the clock cannot
  enable, and the PHY init fails:

      Failed to enable clk 'com_aux': -16
      phy phy-88e1000.phy.14: phy init failed --> -16
      phy phy-88e1000.phy.14: phy_power_on was called before phy_init

  card1-HDMI-A-1 then sits at connected-but-disabled, and probing it blocks in

      drm_mode_getconnector
      drm_helper_probe_single_connector_modes
      detect_connector_status
      msm_dp_bridge_detect+0x70/0x318 [msm]
      __pm_runtime_resume+0x58/0xc0
      rpm_resume+0x4b8/0x6b0

  (that stack captured 2026-10-05 08:57:23).  A compositor enumerating connector
  modes at startup or on hotplug blocks there, which presents as a frozen desktop
  with the kernel still alive.

FIX
  One property on /hdmi-bridge in glymur-asus-zenbook-a16-ux3607oa.dts:

      power-domains = <&gcc GCC_USB30_TERT_GDSC>;

  The bridge (parade,ps185hdm, driver simple-bridge) binds on every boot, and a
  single power-domain specifier is enough for genpd_dev_pm_attach() to power the
  domain on at attach -- with no dependency on the unused USB controller.

      patch  patches/0017-dts-hdmi-bridge-tert-gdsc.patch
      dtb    164631 bytes, sha256 8a9bc3d9587bdabd   (was 164611, 878e76d80eff172f)
