# 2026-10-02 21:42-21:47 — repeated suspends wedge the radio; no hang, clean poweroff

Boot `d92a3795-a1f8-4085-9c5d-69b0d87aa319`, kernel `7.3.0-rc3-next-20260914`, `msm`
`F60D32C034D8487FB39FE42` (patches/0019+0020), `/etc/modprobe.d/a16-ath12k.conf` =
`a16_skip_global_reset_on_resume=Y a16_keep_mhi_up=Y`, patched `xhci-plat-hcd` loaded.

Operator's report: "it started to come up, then flashed, then tried again, then logs, then I
eventually hit the power button, and it tried to shut down. I could not ssh, but the machine was
responsive to some things."

## Three suspends in one boot

| # | requested | slept | resumed | radio after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 21:34:51 `systemctl` (the `sleep test 1` of the previous capture) | 7 s | 21:34:59 | **fine** — `NetworkManager: … Activation: successful, device activated` |
| 2 | 21:42:47 `systemctl` (PID 36484 = `sleep test 2`, attempt 1) | 98 s | 21:44:22 | **wedge begins** — `failed to set ac override for ARP: -11`, `wmi command 16387 timeout` |
| 3 | 21:44:25 `systemctl` (attempt 2) | 99 s | 21:46:04 (woken by the power key) | **dead** — the WMI timeout loop, no network, no SSH |

The wedge, verbatim (resume 3):

21:46:04  ath12k_wifi7_pci 0004:01:00.0: A16: resume -- the device and the MHI link were left up; re-arming the interrupts

`a16_keep_mhi_up=Y` did what it says (the two `A16:` lines are patches/0014's messages), and the
radio still died. The MHI/QRTR device's resume-early failing with `-5` (EIO) is either the cause or
the first symptom, and it is new information: the earlier, single-suspend resume in this same boot
had no such line.

## What the operator saw

* **"flashed, then tried again"** — the resume hook restarted gdm twice (21:44:25 and again after
  21:46:04; the installed copy was the 3-second single-check version). `gnome-shell` followed with
  `shell_global_get: assertion 'the_object' failed` and friends at 21:46:08, i.e. the compositor was
  being rebuilt while the resume was still settling.
* **"then logs"** — 30 `ath12k` lines reached the console in the 90 s after 21:46:04, and with
  `consoleblank=0` plus the DRM fb console those print **on the panel**. That is what the screen was
  showing.
* **no SSH** — the radio was dead, so there was no network to SSH to. The machine itself was fine:
  the power key was seen at 21:46:04 (waking it) and again at 21:47:23, which logind turned into
  `Powering off…` → clean `Shutting down` at 21:47:26. Nothing hung; nothing had to be forced.

## What this changes

1. **The failure is a partial resume, not a hang** — a different mode from the 2026-10-02 20:29 event
   (journal ends at `PM: suspend entry`, hard reset). Both are now characterized; the display half of
   a resume works, and the panel came back both times (via the hook).
2. **The radio wedge tracks repeated suspends.** The first suspend of the boot resumed with the radio
   intact and fully working; the second and third left it dead. That is the mirror image of the old
   xhci "first sleeps, later aborts" pattern — and now that `patches/0010` lets those later suspends
   through, their resume path is what needs work.
3. **Next leads, in order:** the `qcom_mhi_qrtr_pm_resume_early … returns -5` failure (a driver-side
   question: why the autoqueue transfer cannot be prepared after the second resume); whether the
   `-5` also appears on a single-suspend workflow at all (the first one did not); and reducing the
   resume churn — `sudo bash ~/a16.sh display hook` installs the two-check hook that tries the
   session's own power-save switch before restarting gdm.
