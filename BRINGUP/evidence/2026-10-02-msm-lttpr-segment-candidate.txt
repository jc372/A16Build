LTTPR segment training: next candidate built (TPS4 scoping + drive-level capability clamp)
Date: 2026-10-02
Status: BUILT, ABI-verified, NOT staged. No hardware trial yet.

What the captures actually show (two independent runs, same pattern)
- In both the DP-1 capture (`display-outputs-20261002-150232.kernel.log`) and the DP-2
  capture (`display-outputs-20261002-163318.kernel.log`) the monitor's internal DisplayPort
  repeater is trained using **TPS4**, and channel equalization never completes:
    - `link training #1 on phy 1 successful`   (LTTPR1 clock recovery ok)
    - `link training #2 on phy 1 failed. ret=-110`  (LTTPR1 channel EQ)
    - repeater AUX status ramps pre-emphasis 1 -> 2 -> 3 and stays there
      (`0xf0030` reads 0x88 -> 0xcc -> 0xcc), then clock recovery collapses (`0x44`),
    - driver writes `0xf0011 <- 38 38 38 38` = voltage swing 0 / pre-emphasis 3 with
      MAX_PRE_EMPHASIS_REACHED.
- In the same captures, once the repeater falls off (its DPCD block reads all zero and
  `drm_dp_lttpr_count()` returns 0), the driver trains only the sink and it succeeds at the
  same rate:
    - `link training #1 on phy 0 successful` / `#2 on phy 0 successful`,
    - pattern used there is TPS3 (`0x23` on `0x00102`), i.e. `sink: pattern=3`.
- Why TPS4 was used for the repeater: `msm_dp_ctrl_link_train_2()` picks the pattern from
  `panel->dpcd`. In transparent mode the monitor reports `DPCD 0x003 = 0x81` (bit 7 set =
  TPS4 "supported"), and that snapshot is what `msm_dp_panel_read_dpcd()` cached. Once the
  LTTPRs are in non-transparent mode the same byte reads `0x01` (TPS4 not supported) and
  the sink itself trains fine with TPS3. So the repeater segment was trained with a pattern
  the receiver snapshot does not actually support.
- Separately, `msm_dp_link_adjust_levels()` capped pre-emphasis only at level 3 and never
  consulted the repeater's advertised capability (`DP_TRANSMITTER_CAPABILITY_PHY_REPEATER1`,
  0xf0021), which msm never read at all. In the DP-2 capture clock recovery is lost exactly
  when pre-emphasis 3 is driven into the repeater.

Change built (retired/patches/old-numbering/0018-msm-dp-lttpr-segment-training.patch, 4 files)
1. `dp_ctrl.c` — TPS4 is selected only for the sink segment (`dp_phy == DP_PHY_DPRX`).
   LTTPR segments use TPS3/TPS2, which is what the receiver advertises once the LTTPRs are
   active (and what the sink segment already trains with successfully).
2. `dp_display.c` — read and store each LTTPR's PHY capabilities at LTTPR init
   (`drm_dp_read_lttpr_phy_caps()`); a failed read leaves the entry zeroed, i.e. assumes no
   level-3 support (the safe direction).
3. `dp_link.c` — new `msm_dp_link_get_max_levels()` clamps the requested voltage-swing and
   pre-emphasis levels to what the segment being trained advertises; `msm_dp_link_adjust_levels()`
   takes the `dp_phy` being trained and applies that clamp.
4. `dp_ctrl.c` — `msm_dp_ctrl_update_phy_vx_px()` signals MAX_SWING/MAX_PRE_EMPHASIS_REACHED at
   the clamped maximum, and `msm_dp_ctrl_link_train_1()` uses the clamped voltage limit, so a
   repeater asking for an unsupported level is answered instead of being pushed past its limit.

Staging (not run)
- `sudo bash ~/a16.sh msmlttpr` stages only msm.ko for the NEXT boot over the current dpnext
  layer, with its own module+initrd backup under `/var/lib/a16-msmlttpr/<kernel>/`.
- `sudo bash ~/a16.sh msmlttpr revert` restores the dpnext msm.ko and the byte-identical initrd.
  Revert this layer before `dpnext revert`.
- `bash ~/a16.sh msmlttpr dry-run` (non-root) passes and reports the ABI comparison.

What would confirm or refute it
- Confirm: the next watched hotplug shows `sink: pattern=3` (or 2) on the repeater segment, no
  attempt to drive pre-emphasis above the repeater's advertised maximum, and LTTPR1 channel
  equalization completing. That is the first time this link would get past EQ.
- Refute: LTTPR1 EQ still times out with TPS3 and clamped levels -> the remaining candidates are
  the RBR/HBR2-level drive tables and a deliberate lower link rate, not the pattern.
- Unchanged risk: DPU vblank/frame-done timeouts were also present after a successful sink-segment
  training in the DP-2 capture, so link training may not be the last blocker. Repeated hotplug was
  followed by a freeze/hard reset (boot id changed from `42967b08-...` to `4d9da362-...`), so the
  next hardware step still needs the watcher running first and should be a single connect.
