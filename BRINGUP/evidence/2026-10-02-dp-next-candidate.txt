A16 external DP next-step hypothesis and built candidate — 2026-10-02

Ranking from the captured failure (not proof of the reset cause):
1. The A16's internal-panel rate experiment was incorrectly applied to every DP connector. `dp_panel.c` unconditionally set `link_info->rate=810000` and `rate_set=3` whenever the DT allowed HBR3. The live DT allows HBR3 on DP0, DP1, HDMI bridge, and eDP. That bypasses the sink/LTTPR rate cap computed immediately above it. The 13:50 USB-C capture reached LTTPR channel-equalization and timed out; the exact attempted rate was not logged (`drm.debug=0`), so this is a strong code defect and a plausible cause, not a confirmed on-wire rate.
2. A failure-path safety issue could explain the abrupt reset. `msm_dp_display_atomic_enable()` returned on `rc=-104` with `power_on=false`; `msm_dp_display_atomic_disable()` nevertheless unconditionally pushed idle. A posted A16-tested patch in `patches/0008-drm-msm-dp-skip-push-idle-when-link-never-enabled.patch` avoids a reproducible silent reset after failed eDP enable. This external-DP reset was not traced to that path; guard it to prevent a known machine-specific hazard, not to claim causality.
3. The v5 Glymur combo-PHY candidate may still have stale PCS LN0/LN1 drive-level settings during retraining. Keep this for a later experiment; changing it now would obscure whether restoring the sink's negotiated rate helped.
4. HDMI goes through the tert PHY and DP-to-HDMI bridge, a distinct path without a recent successful training attempt. Prioritize USB-C DP-1 because it has repeatable EDID and the most precise failure capture.

Important correction: `link training #2 on phy 1` means channel-equalization phase on DP_PHY_LTTPR1 (the first DisplayPort link-training repeater). It is NOT the system's second physical QMP PHY; `enum drm_dp_phy` assigns DPRX=0, LTTPR1=1. The signal path was DP-1 through `af54000` and `fd5000.phy`.
